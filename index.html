About ME

Senior Full Stack Developer & OJS Specialist

I'm Ridho, a senior developer focused on building strong digital infrastructure for scientific publishing. My current professional direction is centered on journal publishing systems, scholarly communication platforms, and custom solutions built around Open Journal Systems (OJS).
